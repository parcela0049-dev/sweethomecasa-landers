Common questions

Will checking my offer affect my credit score?

No. Checking your offer uses a soft inquiry and does not impact your credit score.

Do I need excellent credit to qualify?

No. Because the credit line is based on your car's value, applicants with limited or less-than-perfect credit history may still qualify. Final approval and terms depend on Yendo's underwriting.

Do I have to give up my car?

No. You keep driving your car as usual. Yendo places a lien on the vehicle's title, similar to how a car loan works — it does not take possession of the vehicle.

What does it cost?

Yendo charges a $40 annual fee and interest only on the balance you actually use, with rates ranging from 24.88% to 35.88% APR based on creditworthiness. Full pricing details are shown by Yendo before you accept an offer.
